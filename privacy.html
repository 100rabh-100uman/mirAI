The Architecture of Privacy.

We built mirAI with a radical "Offline-First" philosophy. We process intelligence on the edge, protecting your sovereignty by design.

1. Edge Computing & NPU

All facial recognition and health analysis happens locally on the mirror's Neural Processing Unit (NPU).

Unlike standard smart devices, we do not stream your video feed to remote servers. The intelligence lives inside the glass, ensuring that your raw visual data never leaves the physical boundaries of your home.

2. Zero-Knowledge Proof

We do not know who you are. Your biometric templates are encrypted and stored solely on your device's secure element.

They are accessible only by you via your face or PIN. Even mirAI engineers cannot access, view, or reconstruct your facial data from our side.

3. The Mechanical Shutter

Privacy isn't just software; it's physics. Every mirAI unit features a Hardware Blocker.

With a simple slide, you physically cover the camera lens. This cuts off all visual input at the source. It ensures that no matter what software is running, the camera is physically blind when you want total privacy.

4. Third-Party Integrations

If you choose to link services like Spotify, Uber, or Google Calendar, mirAI acts merely as a secure display. We use standard, secure OAuth 2.0 tokens to fetch your info.

We strictly do not store your passwords, and we do not have access to your account credentials. We certainly do not sell your usage habits or health trends to advertisers.

5. Encryption Standards

All local data is protected using AES-256 bit encryption. This is the gold standard for data security used by governments and financial institutions.

Even if someone were to physically steal the mirror and dismantle it, they cannot access your biometric profile without your unique decryption key.

6. Right to Erase

You own your data completely. Selling your mirror or just want a fresh start?

A single "Factory Reset" command securely wipes the local flash storage, overwriting your biometric templates and health history multiple times, making the data mathematically irretrievable.
