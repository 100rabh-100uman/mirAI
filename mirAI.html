Privacy & Safety Architecture

Your home is your sanctuary.

Local Processing Only

All computer-vision tasks happen offline inside the device. Even if someone hacks the internet, there is nothing to steal.

No Recording

mirAI's camera works in LIVE mode only. It analyzes visual input instantly and automatically discards everything. No footage is uploaded.

Physical Shutter

A real mechanical blocker. Watch it close automatically to physically blind the camera. 100% Offline.

Limited AI Scope

The AI is restricted. It cannot track people outside the mirror zone. It does not monitor your room.

Under The Hood

Engineering that feels like magic.

Neural Engine X1

4 Trillion Operations/sec

A custom-built NPU (Neural Processing Unit) designed solely for privacy. It processes Face ID, Heart Rate, and Voice commands 100% offline. No data ever leaves this chip.

Infinity OLED 4K

True-Black Technology

Our "Zero-Gap" bonding technique merges the 4K screen with the mirror glass. When the UI is off, the pixels turn purely black, making the technology invisible.

Multi-Sense Array

LiDAR + rPPG + Radar

Clinical-grade optical sensors allow for contactless health scanning. LiDAR enables "Air Gestures" so you can control the mirror without touching it (no fingerprints).

Sonic Surface Audio

Invisible Speakers

No speaker grills. Actuators behind the glass turn the entire mirror surface into a high-fidelity speaker, delivering immersive, room-filling sound.

Hyper-Connectivity
